## Project Description

This assignment was completed as part of the graduate Deep Learning course. Student groups selected a recent paper from a top-tier AI conference, critically analyzed its methodology and experimental results, and presented the work to the class. Deliverables included a presentation and a one-page technical summary covering the paper's motivation, related work, methodology, experimental evaluation, strengths and weaknesses, and future research directions.
